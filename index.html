Adjacent customer examples

Three ways GTM teams move faster with better context

Each example is framed around how internal knowledge becomes more usable inside sales, customer-facing, and cross-functional workflows.

Dell

Seller workflow

Dell rolled out Glean SalesChat as a chat-first experience for sellers, built to bring GTM teams closer to the answers and content they need while deals are active.

How GTM teams use it Find sales collateral, competitive intel, enablement content, and product guidance in one place rather than bouncing across systems.
Why it matters It helps reps move faster with more consistent answers and better context when customer conversations are live.
What stands out SalesChat scaled broadly and paired with agents to improve answer quality inside real seller workflows.

Wyze

Voice of customer

Wyze has looked at Glean as a way to connect fragmented workflows so teams can get to customer signals faster without stitching the story together manually.

How GTM teams use it Analyze voice-of-customer, support trends, and broader company signals to surface themes that should shape messaging and follow-up.
Why it matters It gives teams faster access to recurring issues, customer feedback, and internal context so communication is sharper and more informed.
What stands out The value comes from reducing manual handoffs and making cross-functional signals easier to use.

General Mills

Cross-functional alignment

General Mills has been expanding how teams access internal knowledge through search, assistant, and agents across a large cross-functional environment.

How GTM teams use it Get to the right internal materials, answers, and context faster when sales, product, and marketing all need the same source of truth.
Why it matters It helps teams stay aligned and move quickly when work spans multiple systems, owners, and approval paths.
What stands out The value is in making knowledge across the organization more usable in both search and assistant experiences.

Bottom line: the throughline across all three is faster access to the right internal context so GTM teams can create, communicate, and move with less friction. For Belkin, that maps most directly to buyer-facing materials, internal mockups, and faster coordination across product, marketing, and sales.
